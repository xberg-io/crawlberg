- Almost everything in `e2e/` is generated; do not edit it. Generator-owned files carry an `alef:hash:` provenance line in their header; that marker, not the directory, identifies them.
- Three files under `e2e/` are hand-written and are never regenerated over or deleted: `e2e/node/tests/ssrf.test.ts`, `e2e/wasm/tests/ssrf.test.ts`, `e2e/rust/tests/ssrf_test.rs` (SSRF-defence coverage, self-labelled in the first line). They carry no marker, so this list is the record.
- Keep `generated by alef`, `managed by alef` and "regenerate with `alef" out of their first 10 lines; any of them makes alef claim the file.
- `e2e/go/helpers_test.go`, `e2e/go/main_test.go` and `e2e/elixir/test/test_helper.exs` are alef-owned (`alef verify` reports them FROZEN). Change them via fixtures or generator config.
- CI drift gate: every leg of the `e2e-tests` matrix in `.github/workflows/ci-e2e.yaml` runs `alef e2e generate --lang <lang>` and fails on `git status --porcelain -uall` under its `e2e/` dir (`swift` → `e2e/swift_e2e`). Formatter versions are pinned (ruff 0.16.7, the Dart SDK on the dart leg); read the `~keep` block above the check before changing a formatter or toolchain.
- Generated-code gates here fail open; a green one is not evidence of correct output. Snapshot, `fmt --check` and `tsc` checks pass on broken generated output.
- To prove a regeneration is clean: `alef cache clear`, generate twice, and require a byte-identical `git diff | shasum`.
